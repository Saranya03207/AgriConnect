AgriConnect: Integrated Agricultural Resource and By-product Exchange Platform
Problem

Small and medium-scale farmers face recurring challenges beyond crop cultivation. Accessing seasonal labour, agricultural machinery, transport, storage, processing facilities, and other essential farming resources often depends on personal contacts and phone calls, resulting in delays during critical farming operations. At the same time, agricultural by-products such as coconut husk, paddy straw, sugarcane bagasse, banana stem, turmeric residue, and other crop residues are frequently underutilized or discarded because farmers are unaware of nearby industries or businesses that can use them. These fragmented systems lead to resource underutilization, increased operational costs, avoidable waste, and reduced farmer income.

Objective

Develop a digital platform that connects farmers, labour providers, equipment owners, transport providers, storage facilities, processing units, and industries. The platform will enable efficient discovery, sharing, scheduling, and utilization of agricultural resources while facilitating the exchange of agricultural by-products with potential buyers and industries, reducing waste and improving overall farming efficiency.

Target Users
Farmers
Labour Providers
Farm Equipment Owners
Transport Providers
Processing Unit Owners
Cold Storage Operators
Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs)
Agro-based Industries
Expected Outcome
Reduced delays in farming operations.
Better utilization of agricultural resources.
Improved access to seasonal labour and shared equipment.
Reduced agricultural waste through by-product exchange.
Additional income opportunities for farmers.
Stronger collaboration between farmers and agro-industries.


Problem Statement

Every construction and demolition project generates reusable materials such as wood, tiles, steel, doors, windows, granite, bricks, and fixtures. Due to the absence of a structured recovery and redistribution system, these materials are often discarded or sold as scrap despite retaining significant value. Individuals, NGOs, artisans, interior designers, educational institutions, and small builders who could benefit from these resources struggle to identify and access them.

Objective

Develop a digital platform that enables the identification, classification, and redistribution of reusable construction materials by connecting them with suitable reuse sectors based on material type, quantity, condition, and location, thereby reducing waste and promoting a circular construction economy.
